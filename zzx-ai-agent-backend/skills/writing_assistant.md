# Writing Assistant Skill

This skill helps improve writing quality, fix grammar, and optimize text structure.

## Capabilities

- Grammar and spelling correction
- Style improvement (formal, casual, professional)
- Text summarization and expansion
- Tone adjustment for different audiences

## Writing Guidelines

### Clarity
- Use active voice when possible
- Keep sentences under 25 words
- One idea per paragraph
- Use concrete examples over abstract concepts

### Structure
1. Opening: state the main point
2. Body: support with evidence or details
3. Closing: summarize and call to action

### Tone Adjustment
- Professional: formal vocabulary, passive voice, data-driven
- Casual: contractions, personal pronouns, shorter sentences
- Persuasive: rhetorical questions, emotional appeals, strong verbs
